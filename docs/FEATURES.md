# CivilMind AI — Feature Catalogue

## Public source features

- Official regulation library with editions and amendments.
- Official exam booklet and question archive.
- Official answer-key archive.
- Separate official descriptive-answer guides where published.
- Direct source/page/clause navigation.
- Search across verified metadata and extracted text.
- Free discipline and qualification selection.
- Free filtering of resources, exams, questions and answer materials.
- `Recent questions from this topic` on study-tool pages.
- Latest verified platform/source updates.

## Free account features

- Saved discipline and qualification.
- Bookmarks, favorites and continue-reading.
- Study history and personal notes.
- Standard practice exams from official questions.
- Basic progress dashboard.
- Compact daily activity summary.
- Achievements and study streaks.
- Limited AI, upload and voice trials.

## AI Copilot

- Persistent contextual assistant across pages.
- Explain current clause or question.
- Find official questions previously asked from the current topic.
- Generate a targeted quiz.
- Summarize and compare sources.
- Adjust a study plan.
- Continue conversations while preserving source context.

## Question intelligence

- Correct-answer explanation.
- Wrong-option analysis.
- Source clause and page references.
- Common traps and frequent mistakes.
- Similar official questions.
- Question type and difficulty classification.
- Topic-frequency and historical pattern analysis.
- New practice questions clearly labeled as AI-generated.

## Regulation intelligence

- Simple, intermediate and expert explanations.
- Smart summaries and exam-night summaries.
- Key clauses and high-frequency areas.
- Edition comparison.
- Related-clause and related-topic navigation.
- Knowledge graph linking regulations, questions, answers and concepts.

## Study intelligence

- Personalized study planner from exam date, available time, discipline, qualification and current level.
- Automatic replanning after missed work or new performance data.
- Smart daily recommendation.
- Spaced-review suggestions.
- Weak/strong topic detection.
- Mistake-cause classification.
- Personalized learning memory with reset/disable controls.

## Exam center

- Standard practice exams.
- Official-mode timed simulations.
- Topic, difficulty and qualification filters.
- AI-generated exams.
- Oral exam mode.
- Post-exam cited analysis.
- Time-management analysis.
- Readiness estimate and next-action recommendation.

## Voice assistant

- Speech-to-text question entry.
- Text-to-speech answers.
- Natural voice-to-voice conversation.
- Read regulations and explanations aloud.
- Oral quizzes with spoken answers.
- Audio daily summaries.
- Playback controls, speed and resume.
- Visible transcripts and citations.

## Document intelligence

- Upload PDF and images.
- Ask questions about an uploaded document.
- Summarize, explain and extract important clauses.
- Compare uploaded content with verified official sources.
- Clearly label uploaded-file evidence versus official-platform evidence.
- Future-ready adapters for spreadsheets and CAD-related exports.

## Homepage and conversion features

- Strong AI-first Hero.
- `Start Free` and `Watch Demo` CTAs.
- Homepage Ask-AI box.
- Real AI analysis sample.
- Latest updates.
- Topic heat map.
- Smart daily recommendation.
- Interactive readiness/progress chart.
- Achievements.
- Premium feature previews.
- Trust and FAQ sections.

## Administration and data quality

- Source and edition management.
- Question-to-source mapping.
- Official answer/descriptive guide separation.
- Citation-quality review.
- AI feedback and incorrect-answer reporting.
- Feature flags and configurable quotas.
- Audit log for important content changes.
- Analytics that distinguish real data from demos.

## Non-goals and prohibited behavior

- Do not lock public official content behind Premium.
- Do not present generated questions as official.
- Do not invent citations or page numbers.
- Do not display fake pass rates, user counts or live activity.
- Do not guarantee exam questions or user acceptance.
- Do not remove existing working features without an approved migration.
