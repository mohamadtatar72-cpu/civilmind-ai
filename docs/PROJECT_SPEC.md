# CivilMind AI — Product Specification

## Vision
CivilMind AI is an AI-powered engineering education platform for Iran's National Building Regulations and engineering licensing exams. It is an intelligent engineering assistant, not a PDF repository.

## Principles
- Preserve official documents, questions and answer keys as source material.
- Never replace official text; add verified updates as appendices.
- Cite sources for factual AI answers; label inference and uncertainty.
- Preserve existing architecture and working features unless explicitly changed.

## Access
Official regulations, questions, answer keys, filtering and sources are available to everyone. Premium unlocks intelligence: unlimited AI copilot, voice, tutor, planner, analysis, predictions, PDF chat, knowledge graph and recommendations.

## Experience
AI accompanies the engineer before, during and after study: explain, analyze, plan, track weaknesses and recommend the next action.
